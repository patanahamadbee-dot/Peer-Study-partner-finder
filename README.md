📌 1. Project Overview

Peer Study Partner Finder is a student-focused web application designed to help college students discover compatible study partners based on their academic interests, subjects, learning goals, skill levels, and available study times.

The platform encourages collaborative learning by connecting students who can support each other's academic growth.

Students can create profiles, discover study partners, join study groups, schedule study sessions, and track their learning progress through a single platform.

🎯 2. Problem Statement

Many college students struggle to find suitable study partners who share similar academic interests, learning goals, and availability.

Traditional methods of finding study partners often depend on existing friendships or classroom connections, limiting opportunities for collaborative learning.

There is a need for a centralized platform that intelligently connects students based on compatibility and encourages effective peer-to-peer learning.

💡 3. Proposed Solution

Peer Study Partner Finder addresses this problem through a smart student-matching platform.

The application enables students to:

- Create personalized academic profiles.
- Search for study partners based on subjects and preferences.
- Receive compatibility-based partner recommendations.
- Send and manage study requests.
- Create and join study groups.
- Schedule collaborative study sessions.
- Set academic goals and monitor progress.

The goal is to make collaborative learning more accessible, organized, and effective.

✨ 4. Key Features

👤 Student Profiles

Students can create profiles containing their college, branch, year of study, subjects, skills, learning goals, and study preferences.

🔍 Smart Study Partner Finder

Students can discover potential study partners using filters such as:

- Subject or programming language.
- College and academic branch.
- Year of study.
- Skill level.
- Preferred study time.
- Online or offline study mode.

🤖 Compatibility-Based Matching

The application calculates compatibility scores using shared subjects, complementary skills, learning goals, availability, and study preferences.

Example: Two students interested in Python and Java may be recommended to each other if their skills and learning goals complement one another.

🤝 Study Requests

Students can send, accept, or reject requests to establish study partnerships.

👥 Study Groups

Students can create or join groups for programming, aptitude, competitive examinations, and other academic subjects.

📅 Study Session Planner

Students can schedule study sessions, define learning objectives, and manage upcoming meetings.

📈 Study Progress Tracker

Students can set weekly study goals, record completed sessions, and monitor their progress.

💬 Student Messaging

Accepted study partners can communicate through the application's messaging system, if implemented.

🧠 7. Matching Algorithm

The application can use a weighted compatibility model to rank potential study partners.

Proposed formula:

Compatibility Score =

- Shared or complementary subjects: 35%
- Learning-goal compatibility: 25%
- Availability overlap: 20%
- Study-mode compatibility: 10%
- Skill-level compatibility: 10%

Each category is scored on a scale from 0 to 100.

The final score is calculated as:

"Score = 0.35S + 0.25G + 0.20A + 0.10M + 0.10K"

Where:

- "S" = Subject compatibility.
- "G" = Learning-goal compatibility.
- "A" = Availability compatibility.
- "M" = Study-mode compatibility.
- "K" = Skill-level compatibility.

Example:

Suppose a student receives the following scores:

- Subject compatibility: 90
- Learning goals: 80
- Availability: 100
- Study mode: 100
- Skill compatibility: 70

The final score is:

"Score = 31.5 + 20 + 20 + 10 + 7"

Final compatibility score = 88.5/100

The application can display this result as an 89% match.

This score is a recommendation indicator, not a guarantee that two students will be effective study partners.
